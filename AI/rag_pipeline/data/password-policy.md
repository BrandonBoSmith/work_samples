# Password Policy

This policy applies to every account on the company network, including email,
the VPN, and the ticketing system. Strong, unique passwords are the first line
of defense against account compromise.

## Requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and include a mix of upper- and
lower-case letters, numbers, and symbols. Do not reuse a password from any
personal account. Passwords expire every 90 days, and the last five passwords
cannot be reused.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the VPN, and any
remote-access system. Register an authenticator app during onboarding. SMS codes
are not accepted as a second factor because they are vulnerable to interception.

## Resetting a password

If you forget your password or it has expired, use the self-service reset link
on the portal sign-in page. A reset requires you to confirm an MFA prompt. If you
are locked out and cannot complete MFA, open a ticket and the service desk will
verify your identity before issuing a temporary password that must be changed at
first login.
