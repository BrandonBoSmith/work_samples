# VPN Setup Guide

All remote staff must connect through the company VPN before accessing internal
systems such as the file server, the ticketing system, or any database. The VPN
encrypts traffic between your device and the office network.

## Installing the client

Download the GlobalConnect VPN client from the IT self-service portal. Install
it, then sign in with your company email address and your network password. The
first time you connect you will be prompted to register the device, which links
the VPN profile to your laptop.

## Connecting

Open the GlobalConnect client and choose the "Office - Primary" profile. Click
Connect. You will be asked for a multi-factor authentication code from your
authenticator app. Once connected, a green shield icon appears in the system
tray. If the connection drops, click Reconnect rather than restarting the
client.

## Troubleshooting

If you cannot connect, first confirm your password still works by signing in to
webmail. An expired password is the most common cause of VPN failures. If your
password is valid but the VPN still refuses to connect, restart your machine and
try the "Office - Backup" profile. Persistent failures should be raised as a
ticket with the error code shown in the client.
